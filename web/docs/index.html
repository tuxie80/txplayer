Step 1 · 30 seconds

Drop Your Music Folders & Organize Isolated Libraries

Drag & drop any music folder onto TxPlayer — or click + Folder (or press ⌘K / Ctrl+K → Add Music Directory…). Enable Auto-Watch Folders in Settings → Library & Tags so newly ripped albums appear automatically. Create multiple isolated libraries (e.g., Classical Archive, Jazz Hi-Res) via the sidebar Libraries button.

Step 2 · 1 minute

Enable True Bit-Perfect DAC & Native DoP v1.1 Playback

Click the Signal Path pill in the bottom-right transport bar (or open ⌘, → Audio & DSP):

  • Pick your USB DAC or studio audio interface and enable Bit-Perfect Mode + Exclusive DAC Access (CoreAudio Hog Mode & hardware clock sync on macOS) + Direct 0.0 dBFS Volume Lock when using an external amp.
  • For .dsf / .dff files, enable Native DSD over PCM (DoP v1.1) to verify the gold NATIVE DSD OVER PCM (DoP v1.1) Signal Path verdict.
  • Click Save Device Profile so TxPlayer automatically recalls Bit-Perfect on your DAC and AutoEQ + Crossfeed on your headphones!
Step 3 · 1 minute

Sculpt Sound with Interactive EQ, FFT Convolution & Instant A/B

Open Settings → Audio & DSP to drag any of the 10 glowing frequency nodes right on the live 20 Hz – 20 kHz response canvas (double-click any node to snap back to 0.0 dB). Import an AutoEQ / Oratory1990 .txt headphone profile or load a .wav Convolution IR. Press D at any moment to A/B compare your active DSP chain against bit-identical bypass.

Step 4 · 1 minute

Open the Concert Visualizer Stage & Pause-Freeze Piano Study

Press V to reveal the panoramic visualizer, then press 1–5 (or click the compact visualizer) to explore the 88-Key Precision CQT Piano, Piano Roll, 64-Band Spectrum RTA, Live Acoustic Spectrogram, Stereo Scope & Goniometer, and Mastering VU + EBU R128 LUFS. Press Space to pause on a rich piano chord — pressed keys and the chord badge freeze cleanly for study.

Step 5 · 1.5 minutes

Uncover Collector Intelligence: Power-Search, Audits & Exports

  • Press ⌘F and try composer:beethoven dr>=12 rate>=96 #reference.
  • Right-click any album → Verify Album TrueHz / DR… to persist ultrasonic authenticity verdicts and inline color-coded DR pills into your database.
  • Right-click any track → Start Local Radio (Similar Tracks) for a 100% offline similarity queue.
  • Open Library Insights to drill down via clickable Decade/DR/Composer/Label charts or export a print-ready HTML Dossier or Markdown Catalog.

1. Audio Engine & Bit-Perfect Signal Path

TxPlayer decodes audio on a dedicated thread into a lock-free 3-second ring buffer feeding the OS real-time callback (CoreAudio on macOS, WASAPI on Windows, ALSA/PipeWire on Linux).

5-Stage Signal Path Inspector

Click the live telemetry readout on the transport bar to inspect every stage in real time with a color-coded fidelity verdict banner:

  1. Source & Container: Codec, source sample rate, bit depth, channels, live VBR/CBR bitrate, and RAM Buffer status.
  2. Sample-Rate Stage: Native hardware match, synchronous power-of-2 oversampling (2×, 4×, Max DAC), or Rubato Blackman-Harris Sinc resampling (Fast, High, Ultra / Audiophile).
  3. 64-Bit DSP & ReplayGain: Active processing chain summary or Bypassed (Bit-Perfect Direct).
  4. Volume Stage: Perceptual logarithmic curve, linear taper, or 0.0 dBFS Direct Lock.
  5. Hardware Output: Active DAC name, sample format (F32 / I32), Exclusive Hog Mode, and Hardware Clock Sync indicator.
Native DSD over PCM (DoP v1.1): Streams raw 1-bit DSD64 (176.4 kHz) and DSD128 (352.8 kHz) wrapped in standards-compliant 0x05/0xFA 24-bit I32 frames directly to DoP DACs. If the hardware doesn't expose I32 DoP rates, TxPlayer automatically falls back to its pure-Rust 64-bit Blackman-Harris LUT FIR PCM decimator.

3. 64-Bit Studio DSP Suite & Interactive EQ

Every DSP processor executes in 64-bit f64 precision directly inside the real-time audio callback with zero heap allocations and zero mutexes.

  • Interactive Drag-to-Shape 10-Band EQ Canvas: Drag any of the 10 glowing frequency nodes (31 Hz – 16 kHz) directly on the 20 Hz – 20 kHz response curve (-12..+12 dB in 0.5 dB steps) with live floating Hz · dB telemetry. Double-click any node to snap back to 0.0 dB.
  • Pitch-Preserving Practice & Concert Tuning: Lock-free dual-tap Hann-windowed stereo pitch shifter keeps original musical pitch when slowing down (0.75× – 1.5×), supports real-time ±12 semitone transpose, and switches concert reference pitch between A=440 Hz, A=432 Hz (Verdi), and A=415 Hz (Baroque).
  • 16-Band Parametric EQ & AutoEQ Import: Click Import AutoEQ (.txt) to load EqualizerAPO / Oratory1990 headphone target curves (Preamp, PK, LSC, HSC), plotted together with the 10-band EQ.
  • Partitioned FFT Stereo Convolution Engine (.wav IR): Lock-free 64-bit overlap-add FFT convolver (512-sample blocks, up to 16,384 taps). Choose built-in Analog Warmth, Intimate Chamber, or Mastering Air presets, or click Load .WAV IR… for custom 16/24/32-bit int/float mono or stereo IRs with automatic resampling, Wet/Dry Mix, and IR Gain.
  • Headphone Crossfeed Presets: Gentle, Chu Moy, Jan Meier, and Bauer Studio (700 Hz head-shadow acoustic cross-bleed).
  • Mastering Console: Constant-power L/R Balance (double-click to center), Mono fold-down, Ø Polarity 180° invert, and L ↔ R channel swap.
  • Output Dither & Peak Protection: Off, TPDF 16-bit, TPDF 24-bit, 2nd-Order Noise-Shaped dither, plus a transparent -0.7 dBFS soft-knee limiter.
  • Instant DSP A/B Test: Press D anytime to toggle between active DSP (A) and bit-identical bypass (B).

5. Critical Listening Tools

  • A–B Section Looper ([ / ] / \): Mark Point A and Point B on the acoustic waveform to repeat passages seamlessly. An active golden A–B 01:12–01:28 × badge appears right in the transport deck — click it or press \ to clear.
  • Variable Playback Speed: Switch between 0.75×, 0.85×, 1.0×, 1.25×, and 1.5× inside the Signal Path popover for piano/instrument study.
  • Per-Track Timestamp Bookmarks (⌘B / Ctrl+B): Save persistent timestamp bookmarks for long symphonies, operas, or concerts; jump back to any mark from the Signal Path popover.
  • Sleep Timer: Choose 15m, 30m, 60m, or End of Track via Signal Path or ⌘K with a gentle 5-second volume fade-out.
  • 100% Local "Similar Radio": Right-click any track → Start Local Radio (Similar Tracks) (or ⌘K) to build a 40-track offline queue scored by Composer (+50), Genre (+25), Artist (+22), shared #tags (+18 each), Label (+14), Decade (+10), Hi-Res tier, and Rating.
  • Loudness-Matched Blind ABX Comparison Lab: Right-click any track → Blind ABX Lab → Load as Master A / B (or ⌘K → Blind ABX Lab). Automatically trims the louder master using EBU R128 Integrated LUFS (0.1 dB accuracy), switches seamlessly between A, B, and randomized blind X at identical timestamps, and computes the exact upper-tail binomial p-value!
  • 100% Local LAN Mobile Remote: Enable in Settings → Modules (or ⌘K → Start Local LAN Mobile Remote) to open a mobile-first dark studio Web Remote at http://<LAN-IP>:7878 from any phone or tablet on your local Wi-Fi — zero cloud, zero accounts.
  • Synced Lyrics Drawer (L): Displays time-synced .lrc lyrics alongside playback.

6. Master Library Database, Collections & Smart Views

TxPlayer maintains an indexed relational database (libraries.json, Schema v2) with rolling libraries.json.bak safety protection.

  • 9 Facet Browsers: Switch seamlessly across Tracks · Albums · Artists · Composers · Works · Genres · Labels · Years · Folders.
  • Classical Multi-Disc Album Hero: Dedicated album header displaying large artwork, DISC 1 / DISC 2 dividers, persisted Album DR badge, inline editable Collector Liner Notes, 1-click Album Booklet (.pdf) launcher, and High-Res Scans Gallery modal.
  • Curated Collections: Organize multi-album boxes and themes with custom descriptions and 6 color badges. Drag tracks directly onto any sidebar Collection or right-click an album → Add Album to Collection.
  • Visual Smart Query Builder: Build dynamic rule-based views (Match ALL / Match ANY) over 16 fields: Composer, Artist, Album, Title, Genre, Label, Codec, Custom Tag, Sample Rate, Bit Depth, DR Score, Rating, Play Count, Year, Has ReplayGain, TrueHz Verdict.
  • Live Filter Chips: Instant 1-click All | Hi-Res | DSD | Lossless | ★★★★+ pills above the track table.

8. Tag Editor, Online Lookup & Batch Tag Transforms

Select one track or multi-select an entire album and press ⌘T / Ctrl+T:

  • Classical & Collector Fields: Edit Title, Artist, Album, Album Artist, Year, Genre, Composer, Conductor, Orchestra, Work / Opus, Record Label, Catalog #, Comment, #Custom Tags, Track #, and Disc #.
  • Online Metadata & Cover Art: Fast 256 KB audio hash opens instantly; search MusicBrainz, iTunes Search (+ 600 px artwork download), AcoustID, and your local hash cache. Press ⌘Enter to save immediately.
  • 7 Off-Thread Batch Tag Transforms (inside Tag Editor):
    • Filename → Tags: Parses 01 - Artist - Title or 01. Title directly from filenames.
    • Swap Artist ↔ Composer: Fixes classical rips where Composer is stored in Artist.
    • Strip Track #: Removes leading 01 - , 02. , 1-03 prefixes from Titles and populates Track #.
    • Smart Title Case: Normalizes Title & Album casing while preserving Roman numerals (II–XII), BWV, KV, RV, SACD, DG, ECM.
    • Auto-Number 1..N: Assigns sequential Track # in selection order.
    • Find → Replace All: Replaces text across Title, Artist, Album, Composer & Work.
    • + #Tag All: Appends a custom #tag to all selected tracks.

9. Audiophile Quality Audits (TrueHz, DR, ReplayGain & SACD)

  • Batch Album TrueHz / DR Auditor: Right-click any album → Verify Album TrueHz / DR… (or ⌘K). Analyzes ultrasonic frequency extension above 22 kHz and official Dynamic Range (DR), classifying masters as Authentic Hi-Res, CD Quality, or Likely Upsampled, and persists results permanently into your database.
  • Inline Track DR Pills: Analyzed tracks show color-coded badges right next to their format pill in the track table: DR14 (≥11), DR9 (8–10), and warning red for ≤7.
  • Batch Parallel EBU R128 ReplayGain Scanner: Computes Track + Album loudness (-18 LUFS reference) and True Peak in parallel pure Rust.
  • 1-Click Copy Audiophile Specs: Right-click any track → Copy Audiophile Specs (or ⌘K) to copy a clean forum/notes technical summary straight to your clipboard.
  • SACD .iso Disc Inspector: Open Library Insights → Inspect SACD .iso… to read Scarlet Book Master TOC metadata.

10. Library Insights, Database Health & Portable Catalog Export

  • Interactive Collector Analytics: Open Library Insights from the sidebar or ⌘K to explore Codec Breakdown, Sample Rate Distribution, Recording Decades Timeline, DR Health Histogram, Top Composers, Top Record Labels, and Recent Listening History. Click any bar to jump directly to those filtered tracks!
  • Database Health (Duplicates & Missing Files): Detects duplicate tracks across formats/folders, ranks fidelity (DSD / Hi-Res > Lossless > Lossy), flags the BEST master copy, and offers 1-click Keep Best Quality and Prune Missing Files.
  • Portable HTML Dossier (.html): Self-contained, @media print-ready dark/light audiophile catalog grouped by album with KPI cards, Collector Liner Notes, gold DR badges, LUFS, and #tags. Export the whole library from Insights or the currently open Collection / Smart View / Playlist via ⌘K.
  • Markdown Catalog (.md), CSV & JSON Backup/Restore: Export clean album-grouped Markdown tables, 25-column RFC-4180 CSV spreadsheets, or full portable JSON database backups with 1-click Restore.

13. Data Locations, Backups & Optional Helpers

  • macOS Data Folder: ~/Library/Application Support/txplayer/
  • Linux Data Folder: ~/.local/share/txplayer/ (or $XDG_DATA_HOME/txplayer/)
  • Windows Data Folder: %APPDATA%\txplayer\
  • Rolling Backup: Every save automatically rotates the prior state into libraries.json.bak.
  • Optional Runtime Tools (live status in Settings → Plugins):
    • ffmpeg — Optional; enables lossless file tag writes (-c copy) and playback of .wv, .ape, and .opus archive formats.
    • fpcalc (Chromaprint) — Optional; enables acoustic fingerprinting for AcoustID matching.
    • curl — Used for MusicBrainz & iTunes metadata/artwork queries.
macOS Gatekeeper (Manual DMG): Ad-hoc signed macOS builds can be opened on first launch via Right-Click → Open, or by running xattr -cr /Applications/TxPlayer.app in Terminal.
